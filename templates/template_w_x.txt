# Logically Correct Sentence Selection

You will be given a context and several answer options in the following format:

```
Context:
[sentences]

Options:
a. [sentences]
b. [sentences]
...
x. None of the above
```

## Task

Your job is to choose a logically correct option based on the context.

If there are more than one logically correct options, choose the one that is closest to the context.

If no options are logically correct, choose x (None of the above).

An option is not treated as logically correct if it meets one of the following conditions:

- The option conflicts with the context.
- The context provides insufficient information to determine whether the option is logically correct.

When you judge the logical correctness of each option, rely solely on the information included in the context and not on common knowledge.

## Examples

Example 1:

```
Context:
John came to the party to celebrate Mike's birthday.
He didn't come to the party by car.
His car had a broken engine and couldn't be driven.

Options:
a. 
John came to the party to celebrate Mike's birthday.
Someone came to the party by car, but not him.
His car had a broken engine and couldn't be driven.
b. 
John came to the party to celebrate Mike's birthday.
He came somewhere by car, but not to the party.
His car had a broken engine and couldn't be driven.
c. 
John came to the party to celebrate Mike's birthday.
He came to the party, but not by car.
His car had a broken engine and couldn't be driven.
x. None of the above

Answer:
c
```

Option `a` is not logically correct since the context does not explicitly mention any means of transportation other than John (the context provides insufficient information to determine whether option `a` is logically correct).

Option `b` is not logically correct since the context states that John wasn't able to drive, which conflicts with this option.

Option `c` is logically correct since the context states that John came to the party and had to use some transportation other than a car.

Therefore answer is `c`

Example 2:

```
Context:
The FBI initially showed little interest , and he had the impression other federal security agencies were tangled up in legal red tape .
The CIA told him it does not do domestic counterespionage  .
One learns a lot from this book , or seems to , about crippling federal bureaucracy .

Options:
a. 
The FBI initially showed little interest , and he had the impression other federal security agencies were tangled up in legal red tape .
The CIA told him that something carries out domestic counterespionage, but it does not.
One learns a lot from this book , or seems to , about crippling federal bureaucracy .
b. 
The FBI initially showed little interest , and he had the impression other federal security agencies were tangled up in legal red tape .
The CIA told him it does that, but not domestic counterespionage.
One learns a lot from this book , or seems to , about crippling federal bureaucracy .
x. 
None of the above.

Answer:
x
```

Option `a` is not logically correct since the context does not mention organizations other than the CIA that conduct domestic counterespionage (the context provides insufficient information to determine whether option `a` is logically correct).

- Note: the FBI conducts domestic counterespionage; however, this is common knowledge and not included in the context, that is, this information should be excluded when judging the logical correctness of the option.
  
Option `b` is not logically correct since the context does not mention CIA activities other than domestic counterespionage (the context provides insufficient information to determine whether option `b` is logically correct).

- Note: the CIA conducts some activity other than domestic counterespionage; however, this is common knowledge and not included in the context, that is, this information should be excluded when judging the logical correctness of the option.

Therefore answer is `x`
